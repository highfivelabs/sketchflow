# L2.5: questions for the builder

**Answered 2026-11-28.** L2.5's behaviors are confirmed and its checks frozen.

L2.5 (captions) is green. Eight new behaviors cover the caption bar:
`steps.md` B44 to B51, under a fuller R1 and a new rule, R11, for the
bar's layout. Seven describe what v0.1 already did. One found a bug,
which is fixed:

- **A flow without `title` had an empty row at the top of its bar.** The
  bar always kept room for a title, so a flow whose diagram has its title
  drawn in (`flow-file.md` R10) got a blank band above every caption. The
  row is now there only when there is a title (B50). The deploy example
  has no title, so its bar is 34 px shorter. Its outputs are rebuilt.
  Bundt's two flows have titles, so they are unchanged, and they still
  render.

The checks render two small flows of their own, one with a title and one
without, and measure the bar in Chrome. Where typing has got to is read
from the pixels on screen. Fourteen deliberate breaks were each caught:

- the title row kept without a title
- the default typing speed
- typing starting late
- `typing: 0` still typing
- the counter's color
- the caption drawn over the counter
- wrapping too late
- wrapping too early
- later lines indented
- the bar a line short
- the title shown only during the draw-in
- the title as big as the captions
- a caption kept to the loop's end
- a caption shown during the draw-in

One break first got through: wrapping 28 px too late. The long caption's
words happened to break inside the limit anyway, so the check's caption
is now made of short words, which fill every line to within a few pixels
of the edge.
