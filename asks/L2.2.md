# L2.2: questions for the builder

**Answered 2026-11-27.** L2.2's behaviors are confirmed and its checks frozen.

L2.2 (zoom and focus) is green. Nine new behaviors cover what the camera
frames and what is lit: `steps.md` B20 to B28, plus a new rule, R9, for how
the camera fits. Eight of them describe what v0.1 already did. One found a
bug, which is fixed.

The checks render a small scene of their own and pause it at the end of
each step. They measure the camera from the stage's transform, and they
tell lit shapes from dimmed ones by the pixels on screen. Ten deliberate
breaks were each caught:

- `zoom: all` not at full size
- a different default `zoomPad`
- `zoomPad` ignored
- the view not centered
- only the first shape fitted
- no clamping at the diagram's edge
- no zooming out when the fit is too wide
- the flows' shapes lit on top of `focus`
- badges not lighting their shape
- no ring

The `focus` break first slipped through, so B26's check now adds a flow
between other shapes.

## 1. A step that names no shape (`steps.md` R3, B28)

A step with only a caption names no shape: it has no `focus`, no flows,
and no badges or notes at a shape. v0.1 dimmed the **whole** diagram for
such a step, since nothing was set apart. No flow we have does this yet,
including the deploy example and bundt's flows. But "here is the whole
system" is a natural first step.

- (a) **Dim nothing** (recommended). The diagram shows as drawn, and this
  is what the code now does.
- (b) Dim everything, as v0.1 did.

Answer (Brandon, 2026-11-27): (a), dim nothing.
